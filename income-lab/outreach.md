# Low-Volume Outreach Kit

Use only for individually selected people who plausibly write or release music, and only where outreach is permitted. Personalize the first sentence. Do not mass-send or repeatedly follow up with people who do not respond.

## Direct message: warm contact
Hey, I've started offering a small lyric-polish service for artists who already have a verse or hook written. I focus on cadence, rhyme connections and phrasing while keeping the writer's original voice intact. I'm testing a one-verse or one-hook package for $35, including one revision. If you ever have a draft you want tightened up, I can send you the details. No pressure.

## Direct message: artist you genuinely follow

## Public post
I'm opening a small number of lyric-polish slots for independent artists.

If you already have a verse or hook written but want the cadence, rhyme connections or phrasing tightened up, I can edit the draft while preserving your intent and voice.

- $35 test price
- One verse (up to 16 bars) OR one hook (up to 8 lines)
- One revision
- 3-business-day target after receiving complete materials

This is editing, not ghostwriting a full song or promising a hit. Message me if you'd like the scope and submission details.

## Objection notes
- "Can you guarantee it'll sound better?" Explain the process and scope; do not guarantee subjective outcomes.
- "Can you do a full song?" Offer a separate quote only if willing and able to deliver; do not quietly expand this package.
- "Can I pay after?" Follow the checkout terms; do not begin unpaid custom work.
- "Can I use your edits commercially?" Clarify rights and terms in writing before accepting payment. Do not improvise legal terms.
