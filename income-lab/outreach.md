# Product Validation Kit

Do not sell a product before confirming it solves a real problem. Contact only a few relevant developers with individualized, non-spammy questions.

## Ask a Godot developer
Hey, quick question: when building a turn-based tactical game in Godot, which part usually costs you the most time: grid movement, reachable-tile highlighting, army turn flow, combat resolution, or something else? I'm considering making a small reusable starter kit and want to validate the actual pain point before building it. No sales pitch; I'd appreciate an honest answer.

## Ask someone who has purchased game-dev assets
I'm researching a small Godot tactical-combat starter kit. When you buy a code asset, what makes it worth paying for instead of using a free tutorial: clean architecture, a working demo, documentation, compatibility, or something else? Any red flags that make you skip a product?

## Public research post
I'm considering a small reusable Godot 4.x tactical-combat starter kit. Before building it, I'd like to know what developers actually want included. Which would save you the most time: grid movement and range previews, army turn flow, combat resolution, or a minimal working example that ties them together?

## Evidence to record
- Specific repeated problems
- Current workaround and time cost
- Existing paid/free alternatives
- Exact feature someone would pay to avoid implementing
- Any concrete willingness to test or purchase

Likes and generic encouragement are not evidence of paid demand.
