# Reusable Product Roadmap

Do not build this before testing the service offer. The service is the first-revenue experiment; the product is the low-maintenance follow-on.

## Candidate product
**Independent Artist Release Kit**

A practical, editable toolkit for independent artists planning a single release.

## Minimum version
1. One-page release timeline checklist
2. Short-form content planning sheet
3. Electronic press kit (EPK) copy worksheet
4. Release-day checklist
5. Simple split and credits information worksheet (not legal advice)
6. Distribution metadata checklist
7. Reusable social caption prompts with examples

## Potential price
Test $9-$15 after reviewing competing products and obtaining feedback. These are hypotheses, not market averages.

## Free delivery stack
- Create source documents in Markdown/CSV first.
- Use Canva only if the existing account allows free export of the needed templates.
- Sell via a no-monthly-fee platform if its current terms and payment eligibility fit.
- No account system or custom app required.

## Validation gate
Before building the complete pack:
- Ask 5-10 independent artists which release-planning task they find most confusing.
- Share a preview or table of contents.
- Record whether anyone expresses specific purchase intent.
- Build only the parts people say they would use.

## Success criteria
A product is worth expanding only after evidence of paid demand or strong, specific pre-purchase interest. Downloads, likes and compliments alone do not establish demand.
